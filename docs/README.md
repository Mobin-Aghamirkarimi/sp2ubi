# Documentation

Records that complement the SP2UBI paper and its supplementary material. Section, table and figure numbers are those of
the paper and of the supplement.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `STUDY2-SURVEY-IN-TRAFFIC.md` | Full record of the variant of the speed check with a survey in traffic (summarized in Section S4 of the supplement): design, results on the held-out sites per evaluation variant, the development step with every estimator candidate and rule, and the snapshot-placement check. |
| `EVALUATION-SCOPE.md` | The evaluations of the speed check that were not run, with the reason for each (not printed in the supplement). |
| `DATA-FILES.md` | Columns of the data files behind the detection results (`table_B.csv`, `urban_rows.csv`, `detection_runs.json`; Section S4 of the supplement). |
| `SPEED-PATH-NOTES.md` | Notes on the speed path: radio scope, Layer-2 identifier, re-randomization at release, expected number of records, burst length and beacon gate. |
| `DESIGN-RECORDS.md` | The timestamped design records of studies 1 and 2 of the speed-check evaluation: what each record fixes, its commit, the Bitcoin block of its OpenTimestamps proof and how to verify the proof (the proofs are in `stamps/` of the supplementary material). |
| `SUPPLEMENT-PROVENANCE.md` | For every table and figure of the supplement, its data file and the keys of its values; the further constants of the evaluation with their sources; the SHA-256 sum of every data file that the supplement reads. |
